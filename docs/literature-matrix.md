## Novelty conclusion

The reviewed literature contains each component separately or in pairs: online/adaptive prediction, probabilistic disruption recovery, passenger congestion, graph propagation, and real-time Transformer/GNN forecasting. The defensible gap is the **combination and protocol**: continuously updated calibrated forecasts plus transfer-risk alerts, evaluated under feed latency/missingness and anchored to Japanese public feeds, with an open-benchmark fallback.

The claim must be phrased as “a gap identified in the reviewed literature” rather than “the first system of its kind” until a final database search and supervisor review are completed.
